About us

A studio for
stories in motion.

Khomotso Video Studio provides video editing for businesses, brands and content creators. We shape raw footage into social videos, promotional content and product presentations with a clear message and a distinctive rhythm.

From captions and transitions to audio adjustments and platform-ready exports, our approach brings picture, sound and timing together. Every project starts with your brief and ends with an agreed final cut.

How we work

From footage to final cut.

01

Share the brief

Outline your audience, message, footage and preferred format. Scope, timing and pricing are agreed before work begins.

02
